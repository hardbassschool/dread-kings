## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-01

### Added

- C++23 design-space search and constraint evaluation, robot-model SDF output,
  compiler probing, source-rewrite utilities, and a concurrent council with
  verification checks.
- A Rust companion crate with a concurrent agent council and source checks for
  relaxed atomic stores and TODO/FIXME markers.
- CMake and Cargo build/test entry points and the `dreadctl` command-line
  executable.
